\vspace{1em}

\texttt{talkshow\_guests.csv} has one row for every guest in every episode of six German talk
shows: Markus Lanz, Maybrit Illner, Maischberger, Anne Will, hart aber fair and Caren Miosga. It
has 13{,}747 rows: 3{,}096 episodes, broadcast between 11 June 2008 and 13 October 2026, with
4{,}216 different guests.

\section*{Variables}

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{3.1cm}p{9cm}r@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Variable} & \textbf{Meaning} & \textbf{Empty} \\
\midrule
\endhead
\texttt{show} & Name of the show. & 0\% \\
\texttt{date} & Broadcast date (YYYY-MM-DD). & 0\% \\
\texttt{episode\_title} & Title of the episode (in German). Markus Lanz episodes have no topic title; they are called ``Folge 1369'' or ``Sendung vom 14.09.2023''. & 0\% \\
\texttt{episode\_url} & Link to the episode page on fernsehserien.de. & 0\% \\
\texttt{guest\_name} & Name of the guest. & 0\% \\
\texttt{guest\_role} & Who the guest is, as listed for this episode (in German), e.g. ``\"Okonom'' or ``Bundesgesundheitsminister''. & 1.7\% \\
\texttt{party} & Party of the guest, for politicians of CDU, CSU, SPD, Gr\"une, FDP, Linke, AfD, BSW and Freie W\"ahler. Empty for everyone else, including non-politicians. & 69.3\% \\
\texttt{gender} & \texttt{female} or \texttt{male}. Not reported by anyone: derived from the guest's job title (e.g. ``\"Okonomin'') or first name, so it can be wrong. Empty if it could not be derived. & 4.0\% \\
\texttt{main\_theme} & Main topic of the episode, one of the 21 topics listed below. & 0\% \\
\texttt{theme\_2} & Second topic of the episode, if there is one. & 4.0\% \\
\texttt{theme\_3} & Third topic of the episode, if there is one. & 17.6\% \\
\texttt{theme\_4} & Fourth topic of the episode, if there is one. & 53.9\% \\
\texttt{birth\_year} & Year of birth, from Wikidata. Only available for politicians. & 69.9\% \\
\texttt{birth\_state} & German state of birth, from Wikidata. Only available for politicians born in Germany. & 71.2\% \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

The topic columns were assigned by an AI model (Claude Opus 5.5) that read the episode
description. The topics are listed in order of importance.

The 21 topics: Covid-19; Ukraine war \& Russia; Middle East conflicts; International politics \&
USA; Migration \& refugees; Climate \& energy; Economy \& finance; Social policy, pensions \&
labour; German domestic politics \& elections; Far right \& extremism; Security, terror \&
military; Crime, justice \& law; Health \& medicine (not Covid); Science \& technology; Religion
\& ethics; Personal stories \& fate; Society, family \& education; Culture, film \& music; Media
\& celebrity; Sports; Other.

\section*{Which years can be used}

Guest lists exist only for part of the episodes, and the file contains only episodes that have
one. For other years, most episodes are missing.

\section*{Source}

\textbf{Episodes and guests:} the episode guides (Episodenguide) of
\href{https://www.fernsehserien.de}{fernsehserien.de}, one per show:
\href{https://www.fernsehserien.de/markus-lanz/episodenguide}{Markus Lanz},
\href{https://www.fernsehserien.de/maybrit-illner/episodenguide}{Maybrit Illner},
\href{https://www.fernsehserien.de/maischberger-ard/episodenguide}{Maischberger},
\href{https://www.fernsehserien.de/anne-will/episodenguide}{Anne Will},
\href{https://www.fernsehserien.de/hart-aber-fair/episodenguide}{hart aber fair} and
\href{https://www.fernsehserien.de/caren-miosga/episodenguide}{Caren Miosga}.
Date, title and description of each episode, and the guest names and roles that are listed in the
description, were extracted from the pages as saved on 14 October 2026.

\textbf{Party, birth year and birth state:} \href{https://www.wikidata.org}{Wikidata} (CC0),
queried on 14--15 October 2026, and the guest lists.

\textbf{Topics:} assigned with an AI model (Claude Opus 5.5) from the episode descriptions.

\textbf{Gender:} derived from job titles and first names.
